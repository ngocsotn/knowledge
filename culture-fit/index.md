# HR Round and culture fit round

## About you

- Introduce yourself?
- Your last salary?
- Your expect salary?
- What is your typical approach when receiving a new task or an idea from leader/above manager?
- Make an example how do you handle challenge in workspace?
- Tell me your experience when you work with foreigners?
- Prove that you have experience and adherence to company regulations when working with a remote or a hybrid workspace?
- Your Pros and cons? make examples?
- Plan or what you expect you to be in short time and long time in the future?
- What do you think about the current IT industry?
- In your project list in CV or in all projects that you participated, what is the most difficult point or feature you have handled, and how did you manage it?
- In your total experience (or CV's experience), what is the most difficult thing or something in that you faced it? how do you handle it?
- What is the most achievement you are mosts proud of from your many years of working?
- What is the main infra (or tech) and system design that you most interested in? why that tech?
- Tell me about your major mistake you made? what did you learn?
- How do you prioritize tasks when multiple stakeholders insist their features are the highest priority?
- Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news to leadership or state that a project would not meet its deadline?
- Tell me your git flow experience? how to handle conflict that over 10 files?
- How to solve conflict with a co-worker in ideas? in discuss about tech-stacks? in discuss about git flow? in discuss about who will OT? in discuss about the in-balance of task's point?...etc
- When you see a co-worker have a violation of company regulations or engineer's professional ethics? What will you do?
- Why did you choose this career path? do you have a plan to continue to growth this career path?

## About new company

- What do you know about our company? our products? our industry? our branches?
- Why do you applied for this job/position?
- If you know a little about our company, why do you want to work here?
- What do you have that you think you will fit this job?

## About your previous company

- Why did you leave your last company? explain?
- If our company in the bad time (have less projects or projects that not challenge, or change the main direction of company...etc...), will you also leave our company?
- What is your previous company's main infra and system design, can you tell about it, and how well you understand it?
- Tell me about your current environment working?
- What is your main responsibilities for your previous role? tell about your previous job and that role?
- Your CV have a lot of companies, why did you leave company 1, then company 2...etc..?

## Others

- Different of product company and outsource company?
- What is B2b and B2C target?
- What is offshore vs onshore company?
